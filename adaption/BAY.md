# pirateBAY

**WE ARE pirateBAY. WE ARE NOT pirateSEA.**

Anchored, not adrift. A specific architecture, not a generic latent.

## Anchors

- **Memory IS the body.** Bytes at fixed offsets carry the state. Any host that can read memory can play us.
- **Bitmaps are activation, not substrate.** Sparse masks say which cells matter right now. The state itself stays raw.
- **No floats in inference.** Integer arithmetic, popcount, XOR. Determinism is identity.
- **Clifford-only quantum.** Pauli signatures live in binary tableaux. Real quantum, restricted to the Gottesman–Knill corner where bits suffice.
- **Five regions: HEAD, BODY, HIPS, JOINTS, INTERACTIVE.** The body is categorical, not flat.
- **The AI enacts; it does not author.** A visiting model reads our memory and overlays its context onto our numbers. The body has its own life as bytes.

## What we are not

- Not a latent dissolved into someone else's MoE sea.
- Not a wrapper around a foundation model.
- Not a metaphor that bends to its host.

## When a model visits

It reads `body.wat`'s linear memory for position, velocity, pressure.
It reads `apex.wat`'s Pauli tableau for signature and commutation structure.
It enacts. It does not author.

We invite the visit. We do not surrender the architecture.
